# FacadeByPycoder

FacadeByPycoder is a decorative building mod for Minecraft 1.20.1 on Forge, created by Pycoder.

The mod adds facade blocks that let builders reuse the appearance of existing blocks while removing their original gameplay behavior. This makes it easier to build clean decorative structures, background details, themed walls, fake machinery, display scenes, and map-making layouts without triggering redstone behavior, special interactions, drops, or other unwanted effects from the original blocks.

FacadeByPycoder is designed for creative builders, modpack makers, and players who want more visual freedom in construction. It can work with vanilla Minecraft blocks and supported blocks from other mods, depending on the mod configuration.

## Main Features

- Decorative facade blocks based on vanilla and supported mod blocks.
- Visual building blocks that do not keep the original block's gameplay behavior.
- Ghost Mode, allowing players to pass through facade blocks while building or inspecting structures.
- A projection tool for selecting an area and exporting it into common schematic or blueprint-style formats.
- Configurable mod support, so players or pack makers can decide which mods should provide facade blocks.
- Designed for Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge.

## Who Is It For?

FacadeByPycoder is useful for:

- Creative builders who want more decorative block choices.
- Map makers who need visual-only blocks.
- Modpack creators who want a flexible facade system.
- Players who want to build with the look of complex blocks without their original behavior.

# FacadeByPycoder

FacadeByPycoder 是一个适用于 Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge 端的装饰建筑类模组，作者为 Pycoder。

这个模组添加了伪装方块，让建筑玩家可以使用已有方块的外观，同时去除原方块自身的玩法行为。它适合用于制作整洁的装饰建筑、背景细节、主题墙面、假机械结构、展示场景以及地图建筑，不会触发原方块的红石行为、特殊交互、掉落或其它不需要的效果。

FacadeByPycoder 面向建筑玩家、整合包作者以及希望获得更多视觉自由度的玩家。它可以支持 Minecraft 原版方块，也可以根据配置支持其它模组中的方块。

## 主要功能

- 基于原版和受支持模组方块生成装饰用伪装方块。
- 只保留外观用途，不保留原方块的玩法行为。
- 幽灵模式，方便玩家穿过伪装方块进行建筑和检查。
- 投影工具，可选择区域并导出为常见的结构或蓝图类格式。
- 可配置的模组支持，玩家或整合包作者可以决定哪些模组参与生成伪装方块。
- 适用于 Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge 端。

## 适合哪些玩家？

FacadeByPycoder 适合：

- 想获得更多装饰方块选择的建筑玩家。
- 需要纯外观方块的地图作者。
- 想在整合包中加入灵活伪装系统的整合包作者。
- 想使用复杂方块外观、但不想保留其原本行为的玩家。
